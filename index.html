Privacy Policy for Scoop Patrol

Last updated: July 30, 2026 (added GDPR rights section)

Scoop Patrol ("the app," "we," "us") is a single-player arcade game developed for Android. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it's used.

Information We Access

Scoop Patrol does not operate its own servers and does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to us directly. Data handling in the app comes from two third-party Google services it uses: Google Play Games Services and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

  • Sign-in: if you choose to sign in with Google Play Games (entirely optional — the app is fully playable without signing in), Google provides the app with your Play Games player ID and display name so leaderboard scores can be attributed to you.
  • Leaderboard scores: your level-completion times are submitted to Google Play Games Services leaderboards under your Play Games identity.
  • Advertising: the app shows an interstitial ad between levels, served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). To serve and measure ads, AdMob may access device/advertising identifiers and general app-usage data (e.g. ad impressions and clicks) — see "Advertising" below for details and how to opt out of personalized ads.

We do not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, or any other device data beyond what's described above. The app includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of its own.

Advertising

Scoop Patrol shows interstitial ads between levels, served through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Depending on your settings and region, ads may be personalized based on your advertising identifier and app activity, or non-personalized. Where required by law (e.g. in the EEA/UK), consent is requested before showing personalized ads.

Google's ad-tech data practices are described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising, or reset/delete your advertising identifier, through your device's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads) or at adssettings.google.com.

How Google Handles This Data

Sign-in and leaderboard data is stored and managed by Google as part of Google Play Games Services, governed by Google's own Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can review and manage your Play Games data, or delete it, through your Google Account settings at any time.

Data Sharing

We do not sell any information. The only external services the app communicates with are Google Play Games Services (sign-in and leaderboards) and Google Mobile Ads (serving and measuring the interstitial ads described above) — we don't share data with anyone beyond what those two Google services require to function.

Your Rights Under GDPR (EEA/UK Users)

If you're located in the European Economic Area or the UK, the data handling described above is also subject to the GDPR (and the UK GDPR). This section explains the legal basis we rely on and the rights available to you.

Legal basis for processing:

  • Play Games sign-in and leaderboards: your consent — signing in is an explicit, opt-in action, and the app is fully playable without it.
  • Advertising: your consent, collected through the in-app consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ad-related data processing or on-device storage/access occurs, as required under GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive.

You can withdraw consent at any time — for sign-in, by signing out or disconnecting the app in your Google Account's Play Games settings; for ads, by changing your choice in the consent form (available again from Google's Ad Settings at adssettings.google.com). Withdrawing consent doesn't affect the lawfulness of processing already carried out.

Your rights: under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data associated with you
  • Correct (rectify) inaccurate data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as described above

Because we don't operate our own servers or database, the data described in this policy is held by Google (as part of Play Games Services and Mobile Ads), not by us directly — the fastest way to exercise these rights is through your Google Account settings. You're also welcome to contact us (see "Contact" below) and we'll assist or forward your request where we're able to.

Google's services may transfer data internationally (Google is a US-based company); Google's own privacy policy describes the safeguards used for these transfers.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority if you believe your data has been mishandled.

Children's Privacy

Scoop Patrol does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains no chat, messaging, or user-generated content features. Google Play Games sign-in is subject to Google's own age requirements and protections.
